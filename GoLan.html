Both people talk. Neither waits.

SignBridge turns one phone into a shared space. Signs become spoken words. Spoken words become captions.

Sign to speech

Point the camera at the person signing. SignBridge reads hand shape and movement, builds the sentence, and says it out loud.

Speech to captions

The other person replies out loud. Their words appear as large, live captions for the deaf or mute person to read.

Ready in three steps

  1. Open the link

    No install and no sign-up. It loads in your phone's browser.

  2. Allow camera and mic

    The camera watches the signer. The microphone listens to the speaker.

  3. Start talking

    Prop the phone between you. Signs are spoken and replies are captioned as you go.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. SignBridge runs in your browser. Open the page, allow camera and microphone access, and begin.

Does it work offline?

After the first load, the gesture pipeline runs on your device without a server. Speech captions depend on your browser's speech recognition, which on some browsers uses an online service.

Which sign languages are supported?

List your supported languages here, for example ASL or ISL, and say which vocabulary is available today.

How should I place the phone?

Lean it against something so the signer's hands and upper body are fully in frame, in good light.
